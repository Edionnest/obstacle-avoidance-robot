# obstacle-avoidance-robot
A robot car that moves forward until it encounters an obstacle, then scans 180 degrees using an ultrasonic sensor and a servo to determine the path with the greatest distance to the nearest obstacle and drives in that direction.

## Materials
1. PETG or PLA to print the body
2. Arduino uno q ( or any arduino the size of the uno q)
3. 2 * l298n motor drivers
4. 2 * rechargeable 9v battery
5. 4 * hobby motors and tires
6. M996r servo motor
7. Ultrasonic sensor
8. Battery holder 4XAA
9. 20000mAH power bank (https://amzn.eu/d/053DOSwW)
10. 28 * 10mm M4 counter sunk screws and nuts
11. 10 * 15mm M4 counter sunk screws and nuts
12. 10 * 30mm M3 stainless steel bolts and nuts
13. 2 * clip connector for battery
